- default_burger.webp: Wikimedia Commons, Burger and a Salad (Unsplash), CC0.
- default_tea.webp: Wikimedia Commons, Mug of Tea, public domain / Factorylad.

For production it is still better to replace defaults with real photos from your own buffet.
